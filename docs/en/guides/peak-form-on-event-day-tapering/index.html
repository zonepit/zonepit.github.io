Form on event day: how tapering works

Fitness builds over weeks, fatigue fades in days. Tapering uses the difference: you lower load before the event, fatigue falls, fitness barely moves, and form rises.

Updated: October 6, 2026 · 6 min read

The idea behind it

Form is fitness minus fatigue. Fitness (CTL) averages over about 42 days, fatigue (ATL) over about 7. If you train less in the last two weeks, ATL drops fast but CTL only slowly. The difference, i.e. form, grows. The aim is to be fresh on event day without losing noticeable fitness.

How much and how long

  • Duration: the taper starts about 14 days before the event. For very short events (time trial, 5 km run) fewer days often suffice, for long events (marathon, long-distance triathlon) a slightly earlier start can make sense.
  • Volume: load falls to about 60 % of a normal week, the event week itself to about 40 %.
  • Intensity: stays. Short hard efforts keep the edge, only the amount goes down.

What form to aim for

As a rule of thumb, a form of about +5 to +25 on event day counts as fresh. How much suits you is individual. Try it before less important events and note at which form you felt best.

A taper that is too long or too deep costs sharpness: after doing almost nothing for long, many athletes feel flat, and fitness drops. Form may then be high, but not because you are better.

What a projection can do

From your plan you can calculate how fitness, fatigue and form develop if you complete the planned sessions. Days with no planned session count as zero load. That is a calculation from the plan, not a prediction of your performance: illness, sleep and daily readiness do not enter it. But it shows early whether your form on event day is likely to land in the target range, and lets you adjust the plan while there is still time.

A checklist for the last days

  • Reduce load as planned, but do not stop completely.
  • One or two short sessions at event intensity.
  • Practise sleep, food and drink as on event day.
  • Check your equipment so nothing new is tried at the start.
