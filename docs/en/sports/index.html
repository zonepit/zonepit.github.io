Sports

One week. Several sports. One load.

ZonePit plans your week across all sports. Every session shares the same recovery, so you get one plan instead of several.

CyclingAvailable

The deepest discipline: from power meter to smart trainer.

Measured
Power, heart rate, cadence, duration. Load (TSS) from power, otherwise heart rate.
Thresholds
FTP and threshold heart rate, with suggestions from your best rides.
Plan
Endurance, tempo, sweet spot, threshold, VO2max, with intervals in % FTP.
Send
Via Intervals.icu to Zwift or as a ZWO file (Pro). With 1.0 also to Apple Watch.

RunningComing in 1.0

Running in the same plan as cycling, with its own, more careful progression.

Measured
Pace, heart rate and running power (if your watch provides it).
Thresholds
Threshold pace, running threshold power and running threshold heart rate, separate from cycling.
Plan
Easy, tempo, intervals, long run. No two hard days in a row, across all sports.
Watch
Pace, heart rate and power targets on Apple Watch.

SwimmingComing in 1.0

For pool and open water, developed with swimmers from the beta.

Measured
Pace per 100 m, distance, laps, heart rate.
Threshold
Critical swim speed (CSS) from your best times.
Plan
Technique, endurance and intervals, e.g. 8 × 100 m.

Strength & coreComing in 1.0

Strength, core and mobility as fixed time blocks in your plan.

Plan
Type, duration and intensity, placed so they don't get in the way of key sessions.
Exercises
Come from you or e.g. Apple Fitness+. Completed workouts are matched from Apple Health.
Without a watch
Mark as done, it still counts toward your load.
Everything counts: hiking, rowing, HIIT, yoga and other workouts from Apple Health feed into load and readiness, even if ZonePit doesn't plan them.
