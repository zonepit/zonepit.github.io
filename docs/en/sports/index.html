Sports

One week. Several sports. One load.

ZonePit plans your week across all sports. Every session shares the same recovery, so you get one plan instead of several.

Cycling

The deepest discipline: from power meter to smart trainer.

Measured
Power, heart rate, cadence, duration. Load from power, without a power meter from heart rate (marked “≈”).
Thresholds
FTP and threshold heart rate, with suggestions from your best rides.
Plan
Endurance, tempo, sweet spot, threshold, VO2max, with intervals in % FTP.
Send
The Apple Watch is the default. For cycling and running you can choose Zwift via Intervals.icu instead, per session or as your default, or a ZWO file (Pro).

Running

Running in the same plan as cycling, with its own, more careful progression.

Measured
Pace, heart rate and running power (if your watch provides it).
Thresholds
Threshold pace, running threshold power and running threshold heart rate, separate from cycling.
Plan
Easy, tempo, intervals, long run. No two hard days in a row, across all sports.
Watch
Pace, heart rate and power targets on Apple Watch.

Swimming

For pool and open water, with your own threshold pace.

Measured
Distance and strokes per length, SWOLF, duration, pace and heart rate from Apple Health.
Load
From pace, also from distance and duration. Without it, from your perceived effort – estimates are marked “≈”. Heart rate in the water isn't used for this; it is too unreliable.
Plan
Technique, endurance and intervals by duration and effort, on your Apple Watch if you like.
Threshold pace
Critical swim speed (CSS) from your fastest 200 and 400 m – also estimated from interval sessions, then marked “≈ from intervals”.

Strength & core

Strength, core and mobility as fixed time blocks in your plan.

Plan
Type, duration and intensity, placed so they don't get in the way of key sessions.
Exercises
Come from you or e.g. Apple Fitness+. Completed workouts are matched from Apple Health.
Without a watch
Mark as done, it still counts toward your load.
Everything counts: walking, hiking, rowing, HIIT, yoga and other workouts from Apple Health feed into your load, even if ZonePit doesn't plan them – estimated and marked “≈”.
