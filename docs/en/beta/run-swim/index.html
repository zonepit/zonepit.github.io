Beta · Running and swimming

10 minutes for better running and swimming plans

ZonePit is learning running and swimming. To get load, zones and plans right, I need to know how your workouts arrive in Apple Health: which watches and apps, which metrics, where the gaps are. The beta creates a short report for this that you review and send yourself. Your workouts never leave your iPhone.

Request a beta spot by email

What you need

  • iPhone with iOS 27 or later
  • Running or swimming workouts in Apple Health, ideally from the last 3 to 12 months. Apple Watch, Garmin, Polar, Coros, Suunto, Wahoo or an app, as long as they end up in Health.

How it works

  1. Install the beta: get TestFlight from the App Store, open the beta link and install ZonePit.
  2. Allow Apple Health: open ZonePit and tap “Turn On All” and “Allow” at the Health prompt. Most important here: workouts, heart rate, walking + running distance, swimming distance, swimming strokes, running speed and running power.
  3. Create the report: “More” tab → “Data report for development”. The app searches Health for a few seconds.
  4. Review the report: per sport you see count, time span, typical duration, available metrics and sources. Does it roughly match your training?
  5. Send the report: “Share” → mail to zonepit@icloud.com or as TestFlight feedback. You decide whether and where. The app sends nothing automatically.

If something is missing

  • No running or swimming workouts found: open the Health app → Browse → Activity → Workouts. If they are missing there, your watch or app does not write to Health. Turn on the Apple Health connection in your watch's app (e.g. Garmin Connect, Polar Flow) and sync once.
  • Workouts there but without distance or heart rate: please send it anyway. These gaps are exactly what I need to know.
  • The report cannot be created: let me know via TestFlight feedback, a screenshot is enough.

Three questions (optional)

  1. Which watch or app records your running and swimming?
  2. Do you swim in a pool, in open water or both? Which pool length?
  3. Do you plan your training today in any way (app, coach, feel)?

I use the reports only to develop ZonePit for running and swimming and delete them by the release of version 1.0 at the latest. If you email me, I see your email address; it is not shared.
