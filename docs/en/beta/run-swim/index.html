Beta · Running and swimming

10 minutes for better running and swimming plans

ZonePit is learning running and swimming. For that I need to know which data your watch writes to Apple Health. The beta turns this into a short report without personal data. You email it to me – done.

Request a beta spot by email

You need

  • an iPhone with iOS 27
  • running or swimming workouts in Apple Health – from Apple Watch, Garmin, Polar, Coros, Suunto or an app
  • no German needed: the beta is still in German, so the buttons below are shown exactly as they appear in the app

How it works

  1. Get the beta

    Get TestFlight from the App Store. Open the invitation link and tap:

    Install
  2. Open ZonePit

    Follow the start. Answer the sports question or tap „Später“ (later).

    Verstanden, weiter
  3. Allow Apple Health

    If the Health prompt does not appear by itself, tap „Zugriff auf Apple Health anfordern“ on the Heute (Today) tab. In the iOS prompt:

    Turn On All › Allow
  4. Create the report

    Tap Mehr (More) at the bottom right and scroll all the way down to „Entwicklung“:

    Mehr › Daten-Probe für Tester › Bericht erzeugen
  5. Send the report

    Wait a few seconds, then tap „Bericht teilen“ (share report):

    Bericht teilen › Mail › zonepit@icloud.com

That's it. Thank you! You will get a personal reply.

Feel free to add to your email (optional)

  • Which watch or app do you use?
  • Pool, open water or both? Which pool length?
  • How do you plan your training today (app, coach, feel)?

Stuck?

No workouts in the report

Open the Health app › Browse › Activity › Workouts. If they are missing there, turn on the Apple Health connection in your watch's app (e.g. Garmin Connect) and sync once.

Heart rate or distance missing

Send it anyway. These gaps are exactly what I need to know.

Something does not work

Take a screenshot in TestFlight › “Send Feedback”. One sentence is enough.

Your data stays with you

What is in the report

  • number of workouts per sport
  • time span, month only
  • rough duration (e.g. 30–60 min)
  • which metrics exist
  • watch or app type
  • app and iOS version

What is not

  • name, Apple ID, email
  • places, routes, GPS
  • individual values
  • sleep, HRV, weight
  • device names
  • times, exact dates

I use the reports only to develop ZonePit and delete them by the release of version 1.0 at the latest.
