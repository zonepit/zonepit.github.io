Help make ZonePit better

ZonePit is an AI training coach that runs entirely on your iPhone. Before launch I am looking for 30 to 50 cyclists to try it in everyday use and honestly tell me what is missing.

Request a beta spot by email

How to join

  1. Install TestFlight (free on the App Store), open the beta link and download ZonePit.
  2. Open the app and allow Apple Health. If you have no rides in Health, use “View with sample data”.
  3. Try it for a few minutes (suggestions below) and tell me how it went.

What you need

  • iPhone with iOS 27 or later
  • Rides in Apple Health (Wahoo, Zwift, Apple Watch), ideally with a power meter
  • Optional: Apple Intelligence for the AI coach, an Intervals.icu account for the Zwift route

What you can try (about 15 minutes)

  • Activities: Are your rides there, and are power, heart rate and cadence right?
  • Analysis: Do fitness, fatigue and form match how you feel? Are the best efforts right?
  • FTP: Is the suggestion understandable?
  • Plan: Create a week. Is the explanation clear, does the load fit?
  • Coach: Ask two questions about your training. Are the answers helpful and traceable?
  • Optional: Send a session to Intervals.icu and see whether it shows up in Zwift.

How to give feedback

  • In TestFlight: take a screenshot and tap “Send Beta Feedback”. This also works after a crash.
  • By email: briefly describe what you did, what you expected and what happened.
  • Please check screenshots before sending: they can show your training data.

Your data

Even in the beta your health data stays on your iPhone. I do not receive it. Only what you deliberately send me as feedback reaches me.
