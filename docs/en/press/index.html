Short description

ZonePit reads rides from Apple Health, calculates load and form transparently, plans the week and explains everything with a local AI coach. No account, no servers, no tracking.

Boilerplate

ZonePit is a personal AI coach for cyclists that runs entirely on the iPhone. The app reads workouts from Apple Health, transparently calculates training load (TSS), fitness, fatigue and form (CTL, ATL, TSB), suggests a weekly plan and explains its numbers in conversation with a coach built on Apple Intelligence. There is no account, no cloud and no tracking. ZonePit is developed as a one-person project in Berlin by Alexander Wolpert.
