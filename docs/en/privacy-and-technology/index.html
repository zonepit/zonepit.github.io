Your health data stays with you.

No account, no servers of our own, no tracking. Here's exactly what ZonePit reads, what happens on device and what only leaves at your request.

On your iPhoneCoachApple HealthOnly if you wantAccessibility

Analysed on your iPhone

ZonePit has no servers of its own, no account and no analytics. Your health data is analysed on your device only.

  • CalculationLoad, form, readiness and plan are calculated on device.
  • StoragePlan and settings live in the app and, while iCloud sync is on, in your private iCloud. Resting heart rate, HRV and sleep are read by each device from Apple Health.
  • iCloud syncOn your other devices with the same Apple ID, ZonePit syncs plan, goals and settings through your private iCloud; plan, FTP, threshold pace, weather location, wellbeing and notes end-to-end encrypted – readable only by your devices. Neither Apple nor we can read them. Turn it off under More › iCloud sync.

Coach powered by Apple's Foundation Models

The coach uses Apple Intelligence's language model right on your iPhone. No question goes to an external AI service.

  • PrincipleThe app calculates, the model explains. That keeps numbers traceable.
  • Without Apple IntelligenceAnalysis and planning work fully; changes go through a form.
  • LimitsNo diagnoses and no medical advice. Plus a dedicated safety layer: no weight-loss or diet targets, and a pointer to medical advice for concerning questions.

Apple Health

You decide in Apple Health which data ZonePit may read.

  • ReadWorkouts, heart rate, power, pace, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, body measurements such as height and weight, nutrition; cycle data only if you allow it. The full list is in the privacy policy.
  • WriteWith its own permission, ZonePit saves your perceived effort for a workout to Apple Health. At your request, completed sessions and values you enter yourself (e.g. height and weight) are saved too. ZonePit never changes existing entries.

What can leave the device, only if you want

Only two connections are possible, both optional and neither with health data.

  • Intervals.icuPlanned sessions to your own Intervals.icu calendar, e.g. for Zwift. Workout targets only, no health data.
  • WeatherFor forecasts and weather per workout, your iPhone asks Apple's weather service using your location, approximate or precise, as you choose. We run no server for it. ZonePit asks at first launch; you can turn it off at any time.

Built for Apple

ZonePit is a native app for iPhone (including iPhone Duo), iPad and Apple Watch, not a website in an app wrapper.

  • TechnologyHealthKit, CloudKit, Foundation Models, SwiftUI, Swift Charts, WidgetKit.
  • Watch and weatherWorkoutKit for sessions on Apple Watch, WeatherKit for weather.

Accessibility

Built in from the start and checked with every new feature.

  • In focusVoiceOver, large text (Dynamic Type), high contrast and reduced motion are checked with every feature.
  • EvidenceA full review of every screen is under way before the App Store launch.
