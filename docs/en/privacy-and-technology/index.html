Privacy and technology

Your health data stays on your iPhone.

No account, no servers of our own, no tracking. Here's exactly what ZonePit reads, what happens on device and what only leaves at your request.

Everything on your iPhone

ZonePit has no servers of its own, no account and no analytics. There's nowhere for your data to go.

Calculation
Load, form, readiness and plan are calculated on device.
Storage
Plan and settings live only in the app. Delete it and they're gone.

Coach powered by Apple's Foundation ModelsIn the beta

The coach uses Apple Intelligence's language model right on your iPhone. No question goes to an external AI service.

Principle
The app calculates, the model explains. That keeps numbers traceable.
Without Apple Intelligence
Analysis and planning work fully; changes go through a form.
Limits
No diagnoses and no medical advice. Version 1.0 adds a dedicated safety layer: no weight-loss or diet targets, and a pointer to medical advice for concerning questions. With 1.0

Apple Health

You decide in Apple Health which data ZonePit may read.

Read
Workouts, heart rate, power, pace, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, body measurements such as height and weight, nutrition; cycle data only if you allow it. The full list is in the privacy policy.
Write
With 1.0 ZonePit can save completed sessions and values you enter yourself (e.g. height and weight) to Apple Health at your request, with its own permission. With 1.0

What can leave the device, only if you want

Only two connections are possible, both optional and neither with health data.

Intervals.icu
Planned sessions to your own Intervals.icu calendar, e.g. for Zwift. Workout targets only, no health data.
Weather
For forecasts and weather per workout, your iPhone asks Apple's weather service using a coarse location. We run no server for it. Can be turned off. With 1.0

Built for Apple

ZonePit is a native iPhone app, not a website in an app wrapper.

Today
HealthKit, Foundation Models, SwiftUI, Swift Charts, WidgetKit.
With 1.0
WorkoutKit for sessions on Apple Watch, WeatherKit for weather. With 1.0

Accessibility

Built in from the start and checked with every new feature.

In focus
VoiceOver, large text (Dynamic Type), high contrast and reduced motion are checked with every feature.
Evidence
A full review of every screen follows with version 1.0.
