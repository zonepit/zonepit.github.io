Help

Questions and answers.

Not covered? Write to zonepit@icloud.com.

When will ZonePit be available?

ZonePit is in testing. Once it is in the App Store, you'll find the link here. Features labelled “Coming in 1.0” are in development and arrive with the first release.

Which sports does ZonePit support?

Today ZonePit plans cycling in full depth (power, FTP, zones, Zwift). Version 1.0 adds running, swimming, and strength and core. Every workout in Apple Health counts toward your load and readiness, including hiking, rowing or HIIT.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. ZonePit reads whatever arrives in Apple Health, whether from Apple Watch, Garmin, Wahoo, Zwift or another app. A watch helps for readiness values like HRV and sleep. With 1.0 you can also send planned sessions to your Apple Watch.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no cloud and no servers of our own. Everything stays on your iPhone.

How much does ZonePit cost?

The core app is free. ZonePit Pro with the weekly plan, unlimited coach and exports costs €1.99 a month, €14.99 a year (14-day free trial) or €34.99 once.

Does the coach really run on the device?

Yes. The coach uses Apple's Foundation Models on your iPhone. Your questions and data go to no external AI service. Without Apple Intelligence, analysis and planning still work; only the spoken-language explanations are missing.

Does the coach do the maths?

No. The app calculates every number transparently. The coach explains them, answers questions and suggests changes. Your plan only changes when you agree.

Which nutrition apps work?

Any app that writes its entries to Apple Health, for example YAZIO, Lifesum or MyFitnessPal. ZonePit gives fuelling guidance around training, but no diet or weight targets.

How do I delete my data?

Your data lives only on your iPhone. Deleting ZonePit removes your plan and settings. Your Apple Health data is untouched and remains yours.

Is ZonePit medical advice?

No. ZonePit is a training and wellness tool, not a medical device. For health questions, please talk to your doctor.
