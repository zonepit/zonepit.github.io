What ZonePit does.

What ZonePit 1.0 does. Everything here is already in the public beta.

Coach and weekly plan

Goal, date and time per weekday make your plan. The coach explains why a session is due and suggests changes.

  • PlanningBuild, recovery and taper weeks across all sports, time budget per weekday. No two hard days in a row.
  • AdjustmentOn a weak day the coach suggests an easier session or a rest day. Nothing changes unless you confirm.
  • ConversationAsk in your own words: “What if Wednesday falls through?” The coach is an AI; its answers are generated on device.
  • Free/Pro3 coach questions a week for free. Weekly plan and unlimited coach with Pro.

Readiness

Every morning an honest look at your recovery, measured against your own normal range, not population averages.

  • InputsHRV and resting heart rate as a 7-day trend, last night's sleep score and how you feel.
  • ResultOne word instead of a number: Ready, Steady or Recharge. Plus every value against your normal range and one sentence on what it means for today.
  • MethodBased on the Australian Institute of Sport's HRV guide, openly described. New watch? ZonePit recalibrates.

Sleep

A sleep score from 0 to 100, weighted like Apple's but calculated openly.

  • Sleep scoreDuration against your sleep goal, regular bedtimes and time awake at night.
  • Sleep debtWhat you fell short of your goal over the last 7 nights.
  • BedtimeA recommendation from your usual wake-up time and sleep goal, with Pro matched to tomorrow's plan.
  • LimitYou see sleep stages, but they are not scored: watches estimate them too imprecisely.

Health metrics

Sleep, vitals, nutrition and wellbeing as tiles: a number, a short read-out, a trend. Tap for chart and details.

  • Ranges7, 30 and 90 days, with average, minimum, maximum and trend.
  • GapsMissing values stay visibly empty, nothing is guessed.

Fuelling around training

Energy, carbohydrates, protein and water from your nutrition app via Apple Health.

  • FreeDaily values and history.
  • ProFuelling ranges for long and hard sessions, training vs. rest day comparison.
  • LimitNo calorie, diet or weight targets. General guidance, not nutrition advice.

History and analysis

Fitness, fatigue and form from your entire training history in Apple Health, plus weekly load and time in zones.

  • CyclingPower curve and bests, time in power zones, FTP suggestions.
  • Running and swimmingPace zones and threshold pace for running, threshold pace for swimming, load per sport.
  • Heart rateHeart rate zones and maximum heart rate from your own measurements.
  • Load from every workoutFrom power or pace; without them, from heart rate, your effort or roughly from duration. Estimates show “≈” and their source, e.g. “≈ 38 from heart rate”.
  • Short workoutsChoose from what duration a workout counts for thresholds and bests (default 5 minutes). Everything counts for load.
  • HonestDuplicate recordings from watch and app are detected. Every number is traceable.

Cycle-aware coaching

For women, optional and free. Only appears when it's relevant to you.

  • What it doesReadiness and plan take your cycle phase into account. A higher resting heart rate in the second half of the cycle isn't treated as poor recovery.
  • What it doesn'tNo statements about fertility or contraception, no medical assessments.
  • DataReads from Apple Health and calculates on the device only. It writes only what you enter yourself and allow. Can be switched off anytime.

Apple Watch

ZonePit has its own Apple Watch app: readiness, today's session, logging how you feel and the week with plan and actual. Planned sessions appear in Apple's Workout app, with phases and target ranges for power, pace or heart rate.

  • FeedbackCompleted workouts are matched to the planned session. Sending again replaces the old version instead of duplicating it.
  • Watch faceComplications with readiness and today's session.
  • Without a watchTargets appear as text on your iPhone. Mark sessions done without a watch and optionally log them to Apple Health.

Set up for you

At the start you say what matters to you. ZonePit shapes Today, Plan and Coach around it.

  • Athlete typePerformance and racing, fit and enduring, or health and an active life. Change it any time; nothing is locked.
  • My profileEverything about you in one place, with its source: from Apple Health, entered by you or estimated.
  • Youth modeUnder 16, more cautious progression and no weight or nutrition goals; under 13, no AI coach.

Weather, journal, notifications and sharing

Everyday life around your training.

  • WeatherWeather per workout and a forecast when planning, from Apple's weather service.
  • JournalTag what shaped your day and see how it relates to readiness and sleep.
  • NotificationsReadiness in the morning, a bedtime reminder, a weekly review. Each can be turned off.
  • SharingReadiness, load and workouts as an image in portrait or square format, with a ZonePit or transparent background. You decide what you share and with whom.

Widgets

Readiness, form and today's session on your Home and Lock Screen.

  • Three widgets“Today” with readiness and the day's session, “Readiness” and “This week” with load and form.
  • Lock ScreenAlso as a Lock Screen widget. On a locked iPhone, health values stay hidden.
  • LocalWidgets show a snapshot the app stores on your iPhone. Nothing goes online.
