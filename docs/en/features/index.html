Features

Everything your coach knows.

What ZonePit does today and what version 1.0 adds. Features marked “Coming in 1.0” are in development.

Coach and weekly planAvailable

Goal, date and time per weekday make your plan. The coach explains why a session is due and suggests changes.

Planning
Build, recovery and taper weeks, time budget per weekday.
Adjustment
On a weak day the coach suggests an easier session or a rest day. Nothing changes unless you confirm.
Conversation
Ask in your own words: “What if Wednesday falls through?” Answers are generated on device.
Free/Pro
3 coach questions a week for free. Weekly plan and unlimited coach with Pro.

ReadinessAvailable

Every morning an honest look at your recovery, measured against your own normal range, not population averages.

Inputs
HRV, resting heart rate, last night's sleep and how you feel.
Result
Good, normal or limited, each with a reason.
Sleep
Short sleep over several nights lowers the week's planned load.

Health metricsAvailable

Sleep, vitals, nutrition and wellbeing as tiles: a number, a short read-out, a trend. Tap for chart and details.

Ranges
7, 30 and 90 days, with average, minimum, maximum and trend.
Gaps
Missing values stay visibly empty, nothing is guessed.

Fuelling around trainingAvailable

Energy, carbohydrates, protein and water from your nutrition app via Apple Health.

Free
Daily values and history.
Pro
Fuelling ranges for long and hard sessions, training vs. rest day comparison.
Limit
No calorie, diet or weight targets. General guidance, not nutrition advice.

History and analysisAvailable

Fitness, fatigue and form from your entire training history in Apple Health, plus weekly load and time in zones.

Cycling
Power curve and bests, time in power zones, FTP suggestions.
Honest
Duplicate recordings from watch and app are detected. Every number is traceable.

Cycle-aware coachingComing in 1.0

For women, optional and free. Only appears when it's relevant to you.

What it does
Readiness and plan take your cycle phase into account. A higher resting heart rate in the second half of the cycle isn't treated as poor recovery.
What it doesn't
No statements about fertility or contraception, no medical assessments.
Data
Read-only from Apple Health, on device only, can be switched off anytime.

Apple WatchComing in 1.0

Planned cycling and running sessions appear in your Apple Watch Workout app, with intervals and target ranges.

Feedback
Completed workouts are matched to the planned session.
Without a watch
Targets appear as text on your iPhone. Mark sessions done without a watch and optionally log them to Apple Health.

“Today” widgetAvailable

Form, readiness and today's session on your Home Screen.
