## 2. Daten aus Apple Health

Mit deiner ausdrücklichen Erlaubnis liest ZonePit folgende Daten aus Apple Health:

- **Trainings** (Workouts) mit Sportart, Dauer, Quelle (z. B. Wahoo, Zwift, Apple Watch) und den Wetterangaben, die die Quelle mitspeichert
- **Trainingswerte:** Herzfrequenz, Radleistung, Trittfrequenz, Functional Threshold Power (FTP), Laufgeschwindigkeit, Laufleistung (Watt, wenn Uhr oder Sensor sie misst), Distanzen (Rad, Gehen/Laufen, Schwimmen je Bahn) und Schwimmzüge je Bahn, aktive Energie, Trainingsanstrengung (Apples Wert bzw. Schätzung von 1 bis 10)
- **Herz und Erholung:** Ruhepuls, Herzfrequenzvariabilität (HRV), Schlafanalyse (Schlafphasen und -dauer), Atemfrequenz, Blutsauerstoff, Handgelenktemperatur im Schlaf, VO₂max, Herzfrequenz-Erholung (eine Minute nach dem Training)
- **Aktivität und Körper:** Schritte, Ruheenergie, Größe, Gewicht, Körperfettanteil, magere Körpermasse
- **Ernährung** (wenn eine Ernährungs-App sie in Apple Health speichert): Energie, Kohlenhydrate, Eiweiß, Wasser
- **Profil:** Geburtsdatum (für altersabhängige Werte wie maximale Herzfrequenz und Schlafziel) und biologisches Geschlecht (für Einordnungen und das Angebot der Zyklusfunktion)
- **Gemütszustand** (Stimmung, die du in der Health-App oder einer anderen App einträgst): wenn du unter Mehr › Apple Health als Quelle des Befindens „Stimmung aus Apple Health“ wählst, und – solange „Auch in Apple Health speichern“ an ist – als Rückfall, wenn auf diesem Gerät für den Tag noch kein Befinden eingetragen ist (z. B. weil du es auf einem anderen Gerät eingetragen hast). Eigene Abfrage, jederzeit änderbar; es zählt der Gesamtwert in deiner Tagesform.

ZonePit zeigt sie unverändert an und rechnet damit weder Belastung noch Plan.
- **Nur wenn du die Zyklusfunktion aktivierst** (zuerst erklärt ZonePit in einem eigenen Hinweis, welche Daten gelesen werden, und du kannst dort abbrechen; danach eigene Abfrage von iOS, jederzeit abschaltbar): Menstruation, Ovulationstests, Basaltemperatur, Zervixschleim, Zwischenblutungen

Weitere Daten (z. B. Trainingsrouten) fragt ZonePit erst an, wenn du die dazugehörige Funktion nutzt. Diese Erklärung wird dann vorher ergänzt.

**Zweck:** Auswertung deines Trainings (Belastung, Fitness, Ermüdung, Form), Einschätzung deiner Tagesform aus Erholungswerten und Schlaf, persönliche Normalbereiche, Schätzung von Schwellenwerten und Zonen, Erstellung und Anpassung deines Trainingsplans, bei aktivierter Zyklusfunktion die Berücksichtigung deiner Zyklusphase, auf Wunsch die Anzeige deiner Aktivitätsringe. Gewicht und Körperdaten werden nur als Verlauf angezeigt, ZonePit setzt dafür keine Ziele.

**Verarbeitung:** ausschließlich lokal auf deinem Gerät. ZonePit **verändert keine** bestehenden Einträge in Apple Health. **Geschrieben wird nur, was du selbst angibst, und nur mit deiner Erlaubnis:** deine gefühlte Anstrengung zu einem Training (1 bis 10), verknüpft mit genau diesem Training (solange der Schalter „Anstrengung“ an ist); und – solange der Schalter „Erledigte Einheiten“ an ist, und nur, wenn du in der Einheit selbst „Auch in Apple Health eintragen“ antippst – Einheiten, die du in ZonePit als erledigt markierst, als Training mit Sportart, Datum und Dauer (ohne Energie- oder Distanzschätzung). Solange der Schalter „Befinden“ an ist: dein tägliches Befinden als ein Gemütszustand (Tagesstimmung), gebildet aus dem Mittel deiner drei Befinden-Regler. Änderst du Größe oder Gewicht unter Mehr › Profil › Körper, trägt ZonePit den neuen Wert in Apple Health ein. So sehen auch Apple Fitness und andere Apps diese Angaben. Die drei Schalter („Erledigte Einheiten“, „Befinden“, „Anstrengung“) findest du unter Mehr › Apple Health › Erweitert. Bei einer neuen Installation sind sie eingeschaltet; der erste Start weist darauf hin („liest und schreibt Apple Health“) und enthält dafür keinen eigenen Schalter, und ZonePit fragt die Schreiberlaubnis gemeinsam mit der Leseerlaubnis in einem Health-Dialog an. ## 3a. Abgleich über deine iCloud

Bist du auf deinem Gerät mit deiner Apple-ID in iCloud angemeldet, gleicht ZonePit einen Teil deiner Eingaben über die **private CloudKit-Datenbank in deiner eigenen iCloud** mit deinen anderen Geräten ab, auf denen ZonePit mit derselben Apple-ID läuft. Diese Datenbank gehört zu deinem iCloud-Konto; wir haben darauf **keinen Zugriff** und sehen die Daten nicht. Rechtlich verarbeitet Apple die Daten als Anbieter von iCloud nach den Datenschutzbestimmungen von Apple.

- **Abgeglichen werden:** dein Wochenplan mit Einheiten und Anpassungen, Trainingsziel und Zeitbudget, FTP- und Schwellentempo-Verlauf, der Senden-Status geplanter Einheiten, deine Entscheidungen zu doppelten Trainings sowie Einstellungen (Quellen-Reihenfolge, Zonenmodell, Wetter, Aktivitätsringe).
- **Ende-zu-Ende-verschlüsselt abgeglichen werden:** dein tägliches Befinden (Schlaf, Beine, Motivation und Notiz), deine Notizen zu Trainings, die markierte Körperregion und deine Stichworte je Tag. Diese Felder werden mit Schlüsseln verschlüsselt, die nur deine Geräte kennen; weder Apple noch wir können sie lesen.
- **Nicht abgeglichen werden:** Daten aus Apple Health (Ruhepuls, HRV, Schlaf, Trainings usw.) und daraus berechnete Werte wie Tagesform, Belastung und Form. Jedes Gerät liest sie selbst aus Apple Health und rechnet selbst; ob Apple Health seine Daten über iCloud abgleicht, stellst du in der Health-App ein.
- **Beispieldaten** werden nie abgeglichen.

Den Abgleich schaltest du unter Mehr › iCloud-Abgleich aus; er wirkt ab dem nächsten Start, danach bleiben deine Daten nur auf dem jeweiligen Gerät. Bereits abgeglichene Daten löschst du in den iOS-Einstellungen unter deinem Namen › iCloud › Speicher verwalten › ZonePit. **Rechtsgrundlage:** Vertragserfüllung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO) bzw. für Befinden und Notizen deine Einwilligung (Art. 9 Abs. 2 lit. a DSGVO), die du durch das Ausschalten jederzeit widerrufen kannst.

## 2. Data from Apple Health

With your explicit permission, ZonePit reads the following data from Apple Health:

- **Training metrics:** heart rate, cycling power, cadence, Functional Threshold Power (FTP), running speed, running power (watts, if your watch or sensor measures it), distances (cycling, walking/running, swimming per length) and swim strokes per length, active energy, workout effort (Apple's value or estimate from 1 to 10)
- **Heart and recovery:** resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), sleep analysis (stages and duration), respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, VO₂ max, heart rate recovery (one minute after a workout)
- **Activity and body:** steps, resting energy, height, weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass
- **Nutrition** (if a nutrition app stores it in Apple Health): energy, carbohydrates, protein, water
- **Profile:** date of birth (for age-dependent values such as maximum heart rate and sleep goal) and biological sex (for reference ranges and to offer the cycle feature)
- **State of mind** (mood you log in the Health app or another app): if you choose “State of mind from Apple Health” as the source of your wellbeing under More › Apple Health, and – while “Also save to Apple Health” is on – as a fallback when no wellbeing has been entered on this device for the day (for example because you entered it on another device). Separate permission, can be changed at any time; the overall value counts towards your readiness.
- **Only if you turn on “Show activity rings”** (More › Apple Health, off by default; separate request, can be turned off at any time): your daily activity summary – Move (active energy), Exercise (minutes), Stand (hours), each with its goal. ZonePit shows it unchanged and uses it for neither load nor plan.
- **Only if you turn on the cycle feature** (ZonePit first explains in its own notice which data is read, and you can cancel there; then a separate iOS request, can be turned off at any time): menstruation, ovulation tests, basal body temperature, cervical mucus, spotting

ZonePit only requests further data (e.g. workout routes) when you use the related feature. This policy will be updated beforehand.

**Purpose:** analysing your training (load, fitness, fatigue, form), assessing your daily readiness from recovery data and sleep, personal normal ranges, estimating thresholds and zones, building and adjusting your training plan and, if the cycle feature is on, taking your cycle phase into account, and, on request, showing your activity rings. Weight and body data are only shown as trends; ZonePit sets no goals for them.

**Processing:** exclusively on your device. ZonePit **does not modify** existing entries in Apple Health. **Only what you enter yourself is written, and only with your permission:** your perceived effort for a workout (1 to 10), linked to that exact workout (as long as the “Effort” switch is on); and – as long as the “Completed sessions” switch is on, and only when you tap “Also add to Apple Health” on the session itself – sessions you mark as done in ZonePit, as a workout with activity type, date and duration (no energy or distance estimate). If you undo the mark, ZonePit deletes its own entry again. As long as the “Wellbeing” switch is on: your daily wellbeing as one state of mind entry (daily mood), based on the average of your three wellbeing sliders. If you change your height or weight under More › Profile › Body, ZonePit writes the new value to Apple Health. This lets Apple Fitness and other apps see these entries too. You find the three switches (“Completed sessions”, “Wellbeing”, “Effort”) under More › Apple Health › Advanced. On a new installation they are on; the first-start flow points this out (“reads and writes Apple Health”) without a switch of its own, and ZonePit asks for write permission together with read permission in one Health dialog. You can decline there, turn the switches off at any time or revoke the permission in the Health app; if you decline, the app keeps working and does not ask again, and a button under More › Apple Health leads to the iOS settings; without permission ZonePit writes nothing, and your rating stays in ZonePit only. **Background updates:** With your Health permission, ZonePit lets Apple Health notify it about new, changed or deleted workouts – even while the app isn’t open – and re-analyses them on your device. Nothing leaves the device; you can withdraw the permission in the Health app at any time.

## 3a. Sync through your iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud with your Apple ID on your device, ZonePit syncs part of your entries through the **private CloudKit database in your own iCloud** with your other devices running ZonePit under the same Apple ID. This database belongs to your iCloud account; we have **no access** to it and cannot see the data. Apple processes the data as the provider of iCloud under Apple's privacy policy.

- **Synced:** your weekly plan with sessions and adjustments, training goal and time budget, FTP and threshold pace history, the sent status of planned sessions, your decisions on duplicate workouts, and settings (source order, zone model, weather, activity rings).
- **Synced end-to-end encrypted:** your daily wellbeing (sleep, legs, motivation and note), your workout notes, the marked body region and your daily tags. These fields are encrypted with keys only your devices know; neither Apple nor we can read them.
- **Not synced:** data from Apple Health (resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, workouts etc.) and values calculated from it such as readiness, load and form. Each device reads them from Apple Health and calculates them itself; whether Apple Health syncs its data via iCloud is set in the Health app.
- **Sample data** is never synced.

Turn sync off under More › iCloud sync; it takes effect at the next launch, after which your data stays only on each device. Delete data already synced in iOS Settings under your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › ZonePit. **Legal basis:** performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for wellbeing and notes, your consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by turning sync off.

## 5. Optional: transfer to Intervals.icu (for Zwift)

Only if you set up the connection yourself in Settings can ZonePit send planned sessions to your **Intervals.icu** calendar. Intervals.icu may then pass these sessions on to Zwift if you have enabled that there. The default target when sending is the Apple Watch on iPhone (section 7) and Zwift on iPad; Intervals.icu and Zwift are available only for cycling and running, and only once the connection is set up.

- **Sent per session:** date, session name, duration, interval structure (as % of your FTP), an estimated training load (TSS) and a technical identifier.
- **Coaching cues:** if "Coaching texts" is on (default, can be turned off under More › Send to), the description contains short, general cues per segment (e.g. "relax your shoulders"). They are generated from the session, not from your health data. **Your first name** is included only if you have explicitly turned on "Use my name".
- **Not sent:** heart rate, power recordings, weight, vital signs, wellbeing or any other health data from Apple Health.
- **Retrieved:** your athlete name and FTP from Intervals.icu, to verify the connection and flag differences.
- Your **API key** is stored only in this device's iOS Keychain.
- Transfers happen only when you trigger them ("Send to Intervals.icu").

**Legal basis:** your consent by setting up and triggering the transfer (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). The operators of Intervals.icu and Zwift are responsible for processing on their side; their privacy policies apply. You can disconnect and delete the key in Settings at any time.

## 6. Optional: weather

At first launch ZonePit asks whether you want to use the weather feature; the switch is off by default, and you can turn it on there or later under More › Permissions. The feature only becomes active once you then allow location access in the iOS dialog. ZonePit then shows the weather for your workouts and a forecast for planning. For this your iPhone queries Apple's weather service (WeatherKit) with your location. **You choose how precise:** an approximate location (such as your city) or your precise location; you can change this in ZonePit and in iOS Location Services at any time. The location is used only for the request. We run no server of our own for this and receive neither your location nor the weather data. No health data is transmitted. ZonePit shows the attributions Apple requires (the “Weather” mark and a link to further data sources) next to the weather information and under More › Weather › Data sources.

**Legal basis:** your consent, given by allowing location access in the iOS dialog (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without this permission no location is queried. Apple's privacy policy applies to processing by Apple. You can turn the feature off at any time in the settings. Weather details that a recording app stores in Apple Health itself (section 2) are read by ZonePit regardless.

## 7. Export, sharing and widgets

When you export a session as a ZWO file, the app creates the file locally. You decide where it goes via the iOS share sheet.

**Sending to Apple Watch:** When you send a session to your Apple Watch, ZonePit hands it to the Workout app on your watch via Apple's workout interface (WorkoutKit). Apple's system handles the transfer between iPhone and watch; nothing is sent to us or to another service. The “Zwift” target (section 5) is separate and selectable only for cycling and running.

**ZonePit on Apple Watch:** The ZonePit app on your watch shows readiness, today's session and the week with plan and actual. Your iPhone calculates this information and transfers it over Apple's direct connection between iPhone and paired watch (WatchConnectivity) – nothing is sent to us or to any other service. The watch app does not read from Apple Health itself and does not calculate anything. If you log how you feel on the watch, the entry travels back to your iPhone the same way and is treated there like an entry in the app (section 3). On the watch face (complications), the watch hides health values while it is locked.

**Share cards:** You can share readiness, load or a workout as an image. The app creates the image on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to any other service of the app. You decide what the image shows and who receives it via the iOS share sheet. ZonePit needs no additional Health permission for this.

**Widgets:** The widgets on your Home Screen and Lock Screen show a local snapshot that the app stores on your iPhone. It is shared between the app and the widget only through an App Group (a shared, protected storage on the device) and does not leave the iPhone. The widgets do not read from Apple Health themselves. On a locked iPhone they hide health values such as readiness, HRV and resting heart rate and show placeholders instead. You can remove widgets at any time.

**Data sample for testers (beta only):** In test versions via TestFlight you can voluntarily create a data sample under More › For testers. It contains only counts and shares (such as how many workouts per sport have heart rate or power), no individual values, no names, no locations, and time periods only to the month; cycle data, weight, height, age and nutrition are not included. The app shows you the full sample before you send it yourself via the iOS share sheet. Nothing is transmitted automatically. We use what you send us only to improve ZonePit (legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) and delete it on request.
